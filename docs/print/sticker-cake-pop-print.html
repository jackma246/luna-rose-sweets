Vanilla Birthday Cake Pop - ingredients QR sticker

Links to https://dipsprinkle.com/ingredients/cake-pop

3 in circle - 6 per US Letter sheet - QR is version 4, ECC level Q

Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") or the code shrinks below the size it was verified at. Cut on the outside of the cocoa ring. The cream centre must stay light and matte - a glossy or dark laminate over the code is the usual reason a sticker stops scanning.
